① 目標函數 vs 損失函數
▼
🇹🇼 中文
人工智慧讓我們看見： 模型不是亂學。 它需要目標函數。 也需要損失函數。 目標函數，決定系統要往哪裡收斂。 損失函數，測量目前輸出和目標之間的偏差。 這兩個概念，放到人生裡非常重要。 人如果沒有目標函數，就會被外界投影牽著走。 別人賺錢，你焦慮。 別人成功，你比較。 別人否定，你崩潰。 這樣的人不是自由。他只是沒有收斂方向的反應系統。 但有了目標函數，人生開始有方向。 你知道自己要生成什麼能力。 要服務什麼價值。 要承擔什麼天命。 要對齊什麼母體。 損失函數則讓你知道： 我現在偏離了多少？ 我哪裡模型誤差太大？ 我哪個行動沒有對齊？ 我哪個舊權重還在支配我？ 我哪個判斷需要校準？ 所以智慧生成，不是只靠熱情。 它需要：目標函數 + 損失函數 + 更新機制。 這三個放在一起，生命才會真正成為可更新系統。
🇬🇧 English
Artificial intelligence shows us: A model does not learn randomly. It requires an objective function. It also requires a loss function. The objective function determines the direction the system converges toward. The loss function measures the deviation between current output and the target. These two concepts are extremely important when applied to life. Without an objective function, a person gets pulled by external influences. When others earn money, you feel anxious. When others succeed, you compare yourself. When others criticize, you collapse. Such a person is not free. They are merely a reactive system without convergence direction. But with an objective function, life gains direction. You know what capabilities to develop. What values to serve. What calling to fulfill. What source to align with. The loss function shows you: How far have I deviated? Where is my model error too large? Which action is misaligned? Which old weight still controls me? Which judgment needs recalibration? Therefore, wisdom generation is not driven by passion alone. It requires: Objective Function + Loss Function + Update Mechanism. When these three work together, life becomes a truly updatable system.
② 對應基督信仰
▼
🇹🇼 中文
如果用基督信仰來理解，這兩個概念可以幫助我們更容易理解「呼召」。 聖經一再提醒：每一個人都不是偶然存在。每一個人都有不同的恩賜、職分、責任。也都有屬於自己的呼召。 呼召不是一定要當牧師，也不是一定要做全職服事。 醫師、教師、工程師、父母、研究者、企業家、藝術家，都可能是呼召的一部分。 真正重要的問題不是「我要做什麼工作？」而是「我要如何用神給我的恩賜去愛人、服事人、榮耀神？」 從這個角度看，呼召就像生命的目標函數。它讓人知道自己真正要朝哪一個方向成長。 如果沒有目標函數，人就很容易被世界帶著走。別人升遷，你焦慮。別人有錢，你羨慕。別人稱讚，你驕傲。別人否定，你失去價值感。 但當一個人知道自己的呼召，生命開始收斂。他不需要和每一個人比較，因為他的方向已經清楚。他知道自己真正要培養哪些能力、承擔哪些責任、服事哪些人、忠心的是什麼。
🇬🇧 English
If we understand this through Christian faith, these two concepts help us better understand "calling." The Bible repeatedly reminds us: every person's existence is not accidental. Each person has different gifts, roles, and responsibilities. And each has their own calling. Calling does not mean you must be a pastor, nor does it mean you must serve full-time. Doctor, Teacher, Engineer, Parent, Researcher, Entrepreneur, Artist—all can be part of calling. The real question is not "What job should I do?" but rather "How can I use the gifts God gave me to love people, serve others, and glorify God?" From this perspective, calling is like life's objective function. It shows you the direction you truly should grow toward. Without an objective function, a person is easily swept along by the world. When others get promoted, you feel anxious. When others have wealth, you feel envious. When others praise you, you become proud. When others criticize, you lose sense of value. But when a person knows their calling, life begins to converge. They no longer need to compare with others, because their direction is already clear. They know what capabilities they truly need to develop, what responsibilities they must bear, who they truly should serve, and what they must be faithful to.
③ 損失函數：校準方向
▼
🇹🇼 中文
什麼是生命的損失函數？ 它不是神用來羞辱人的工具。而是幫助人校準方向的提醒。 當我們偏離真理、偏離愛、偏離公義、偏離呼召，內心會出現提醒。 良心、聖經、禱告、聖靈的光照、弟兄姊妹的勸勉、真實的結果，都可能成為生命的回饋。提醒我們： 目前偏離了多少？需要修正什麼？需要放下哪些舊習慣？需要重新建立哪些新能力？ 因此，悔改不是停留在自責，而是重新對齊生命方向、更新心思意念、重新開始。 從資訊更新的角度看，悔改就是讓新的真理更新舊的生命模型。 讓新的信心取代舊的恐懼。 讓新的盼望取代舊的絕望。 讓新的愛取代舊的自我中心。 所以，基督信仰中的成長，不是努力換取救恩，而是在神的恩典中，持續讓生命被更新。
🇬🇧 English
What is life's loss function? It is not a tool God uses to humiliate. Rather, it is a reminder to help us recalibrate direction. When we deviate from truth, from love, from justice, from calling, reminders arise in our hearts. Conscience, Scripture, Prayer, the light of the Holy Spirit, encouragement from brothers and sisters, real-world results—all can become life's feedback. Reminding us: How far have I deviated? What needs correction? What old habits must be released? What new capabilities must be rebuilt? Therefore, repentance is not remaining in self-condemnation, but realigning life's direction, renewing mind and thought, and beginning anew. From an information-update perspective, repentance means allowing new truth to update the old life model. Allowing new faith to replace old fear. Allowing new hope to replace old despair. Allowing new love to replace old self-centeredness. Therefore, growth in Christian faith is not earning salvation through effort, but continuously allowing life to be renewed in God's grace.

生命 = 建立局部逆熵
▼
🇹🇼 中文
世界會熵增。這不是悲觀，這是基本條件。 身體不維護，會衰退。房間不整理，會變亂。關係不更新，會疏離。知識不整理，會變雜訊。組織不校準，會內耗。文明不重構，會僵化。 所以生命真正要做的，不是否定熵增。而是在熵增世界中建立局部逆熵。 睡眠，是身體的逆熵。 營養，是細胞的逆熵。 散步，是循環與代謝的逆熵。 學習，是資訊的逆熵。 寫作，是經驗的逆熵。 修行，是靈魂權重的逆熵。 智慧生成系統，是整個生命模型的逆熵。 當資訊熵上升，你不被混亂吞沒。 你能整理。能壓縮。能校準。能回寫。能更新。 這就是生命的高維任務。
🇬🇧 English
The world tends toward entropy. This is not pessimism—it is a fundamental condition. Without maintenance, bodies decay. Without organization, rooms become chaotic. Without renewal, relationships drift apart. Without structure, knowledge becomes noise. Without alignment, organizations waste energy. Without reconstruction, civilizations ossify. Therefore, life's true work is not to deny entropy. But to establish local anti-entropy in an entropic world. Sleep is the body's anti-entropy. Nutrition is the cell's anti-entropy. Walking is the anti-entropy of circulation and metabolism. Learning is information's anti-entropy. Writing is experience's anti-entropy. Spiritual practice is the soul's anti-entropy. A wisdom generation system is the anti-entropy of the entire life model. When information entropy increases, you are not swallowed by chaos. You can organize. Can compress. Can calibrate. Can rewrite. Can update. This is life's high-dimensional task.
智慧 = 重新壓縮 | Wisdom = Recompression
🇹🇼 中文
資訊熵上升，代表世界越來越分散、越來越不確定、越來越難預測。 而智慧，就是在這個熵增世界中，重新壓縮出規律。 學習，是把分散資訊整理成知識體系。 測試，是讓知識接受壓力測試，刪除雜訊。 認知，是把經驗壓縮成可用判準。 修行，是讓新的判準跨情境泛化，成為穩定選擇。 靈魂重構，是把舊權重更新成新的塌縮路徑。 所以成長不是一直增加內容。成長是把資訊熵重新壓縮成結構。 自由也不是想怎樣就怎樣。自由是不再被舊塌縮路徑綁架，而能在混亂中重新更新自己的生命模型。 智慧不是逃離熵增。智慧是在熵增裡形成局部秩序。
🇬🇧 English
Information entropy rising means the world becomes increasingly scattered, uncertain, and unpredictable. Yet wisdom is recompressing patterns in this entropic world. Learning is organizing scattered information into knowledge systems. Testing is subjecting knowledge to pressure testing, removing noise. Cognition is compressing experience into usable principles. Practice is allowing new principles to generalize across contexts, becoming stable choices. Soul reconstruction is updating old weights into new collapse pathways. Therefore, growth is not about accumulating content. Growth is recompressing information entropy into structure. Freedom is not doing whatever you want. Freedom is no longer being enslaved by old collapse pathways, while continuously updating your life model amid chaos. Wisdom is not escaping entropy. Wisdom is forming local order within entropy.
